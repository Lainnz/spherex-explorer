What is SPHEREx?

SPHEREx is a NASA space telescope launched in 2025. It maps the whole sky in 102 colours of infrared light, invisible to our eyes, and comes back to each spot about every six months.

Because it keeps returning, its images show the sky changing: nearby stars and brown dwarfs creeping across the sky, and stars that brighten and fade.

How to use

  1. Type a star name (like Barnard's Star) or sky coordinates, then press EXPLORE.
  2. Or press Randomize to visit a surprise spot.
  3. In the Stellar View, play the time slider to watch the SPHEREx images like a short video.

Coordinates can be decimal degrees (269.452 4.693) or hours and degrees (17h57m48.97s +04d41m36.1s).
