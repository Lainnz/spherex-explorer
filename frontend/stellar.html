About this view

You're looking out from the centre of the Earth with north (the celestial pole) up, like Stellarium without the ground. The background is the SPHEREx all-sky map; outlines show individual SPHEREx images of this spot.

Sky viewer: Aladin Lite (CDS, Strasbourg). Data: NASA SPHEREx via IRSA, ESA Gaia, NASA WISE, 2MASS.

Show

SPHEREx (NASA) maps the sky in 102 infrared colours. Gaia (ESA) measures star positions, distances and motions. WISE (NASA) mapped the infrared sky from 2010. 2MASS is a near-infrared sky survey.
